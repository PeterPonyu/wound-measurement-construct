# Methodological contract


The frozen expression model uses a diagonal-Gaussian encoder, a softmax transform and a row-normalized topic-gene decoder. Its loss is equal-cell cross-entropy on panel proportions plus 0.01 times Gaussian KL to a standard normal. It is a logistic-normal regularized representation, not an exact Dirichlet model, an unmodified count-likelihood ELBO or a calibrated classifier of biological states. Deterministic projection uses softmax of the Gaussian mean, not the posterior mean of the softmax.

Three estimands must remain separate: mean topic loading within gated fibroblasts, the fraction of those fibroblasts above a loading cut, and the mean topic loading over all retained cells from a mapped patient. The latter is the primary healing contrast. Repeated specimens are pooled by retained cell count within patient; patients then receive equal weight within each outcome arm. The outcome subset has 11 patients, not 14 independent specimens or thousands of independent cells.

The threshold mean identity is exact even when one bin is empty, provided its weighted contribution is zero. A correlation between the mean and threshold fraction does not imply constant bin means, interchangeable estimands, two biological attractors or a causal abundance effect. The full fitted cut 0.184295848 and historical rounded technical-control cut 0.184 have distinct documented uses. The new frozen-score analysis measures the residual of a stated constant-bin approximation and excludes undefined empty-bin means from their ranges.

The current deterministic patient projection gives an all-cell mean difference of 0.029279668 and 90 extreme allocations among all 330 allocations: exhaustive p=90/330=0.272727 and retained add-one p=91/331=0.274924. The earlier 89/330 and 90/331 values belong to a superseded projection and are retained only in historical reports. The source is outputs/scientific_revision_20260922/measurement/mathematical_audit/report.json. Interpretation assumes observational label exchangeability. The 90% Welch interval and two one-sided equivalence tests agree algebraically; their data-derived boundary does not supply a clinical equivalence margin.

Pooled raw out-of-fold PCA/NMF coordinates have fold-dependent origins, scales and components. Their historical AUCs and fixed-score bootstrap intervals describe those scoring procedures and cannot rank representation quality. Fixed-score bootstraps also exclude learning-algorithm uncertainty. Discovery representation fitting includes expression from subsequently held-out patients, so discrimination remains internal even when a new scoring protocol corrects cross-fold comparability.

The revised patient-pair audit holds one healed and one non-healed patient out together and trains on the remaining nine. The same fitted rule scores both held patients. Across all 28 dependent pairs, topic, module, PCA and NMF pair discrimination is 0.714286, 0.785714, 0.500000 and 0.232143. NMF uses a normalized training basis, independent row-wise NNLS projection and a training-SD-standardized component contrast. Its iteration cap was increased uniformly from 1,000 to 10,000 after three training-convergence warnings, without selecting by held-pair performance; the pilot is preserved and final fits have no warnings. All 28 pairs reuse 11 patients. No new p values, pair-binomial uncertainty or external representation validation is claimed. Source: outputs/scientific_revision_20260923/patient_pair_discrimination/report.json.

Frozen-reference marker assignment is reproducible within the same reference population; re-estimating marker normalization by specimen changes 7,044 of 76,461 labels. This sensitivity does not establish a portable gate. The 99.5% depth-control agreement is conditional on retaining the higher-depth cells and keeping their lineage labels fixed; it does not measure the stability of the complete QC, gate and score pipeline. Repeated same-wound specimens expose additional tissue-sampling variability.
