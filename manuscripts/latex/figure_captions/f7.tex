\paperfigure{figure7_representation_inference}{fig:p1f7}{Historical scoring diagnostics in 14 DFU specimens (9 healing, 5 non-healing). (A) Leave-one-sample-out ROC areas (AUCs) with percentile 95\% intervals from 4,000 arm-stratified bootstrap draws of fixed out-of-fold scores, using common resampling indices. Orange ticks: permutation-null means after rerunning outcome-dependent fitting and orientation; dashed line: 0.5, not the fitted null. Fibroblast topic is the frozen all-cell mean; Module is the specified fibro-inflammatory score; PCA and NMF denote principal component and nonnegative matrix readouts. Different fitted-fold coordinates and repeated patients prevent these pooled AUCs or intervals from supporting a representation ranking. (B) Spearman correlations of the same 14 specimen readouts; colours and numbers encode the same statistic. These are procedure diagnostics, not independent patient validation.}
