\paperfigure{figure1_workflow}{fig:p1f1}{Study design and measurement logic. (A) The discovery series supplies 76,461 retained cells from 25 selected foot-skin specimens. A frozen 15-topic expression model produces three distinct summaries: the mean fibroblast loading, the fraction of fibroblasts above a fixed loading cut, and the all-cell mean within each mapped patient. The gene labels describe leading decoder genes, not a newly defined cell type. (B) The cell cartoons are conceptual, not observed counts: grey and teal circles denote low and high states. Changing their relative abundance can increase the sample mean without changing either state's intensity; a mean--fraction correlation alone cannot establish constant within-state expression. (C) Technical perturbations test robustness, the healing contrast collapses 14 DFU specimens into 11 patients, paired anatomy tests tissue sensitivity, and external projections assess cohort-specific construct associations. Each evidence layer addresses a different question; these analyses do not supply independent prognostic validation.}
