\section{Discussion}
Mean fibroblast loading and the fraction of high-scoring fibroblasts were closely associated across the discovery specimens, and the association persisted after patient aggregation. This establishes that the mean conveys information about the prevalence of high scores in the recovered cells. It does not make the two measurements interchangeable. The low- and high-bin means varied across specimens, and nine specimens contained no cells in the high bin. A constant-state approximation retained a nonzero specimen-level residual. The appropriate interpretation is therefore a mixture of prevalence and within-bin score variation, conditional on the chosen representation and threshold.

This reading is consistent with the distinction between cell identity, cellular activity and tissue composition \citep{kotliar2019,buettner2021}. A reproducible expression loading need not identify a discrete biological state, particularly when a continuous bounded score is summarized by a two-Gaussian fit. The threshold provides an operational measurement, not evidence that fibroblasts occupy two stable attractors. Spatial measurements of cell abundance, independent protein or transcript assays and repeated tissue sampling would help determine how much of the observed variation reflects tissue organization, cell activity or recovery during processing.

The technical controls narrow some explanations without resolving all of them. Most state labels survived count matching, and specimen summaries were stable after excluding cells carrying specified immune transcripts. Those observations support robustness to the tested perturbations. They cannot establish the absence of ambient RNA or doublets, whose effects depend on the source of contamination and the expression profiles of affected cells \citep{vandenbrink2017,young2020}. The similar attenuation of residual bimodality by immune and unrelated tissue covariates further limits an interpretation specific to one immune mechanism.

The clinical evidence is more limited than the measurement association. The highest threshold fraction occurred in healthy skin, challenging disease exclusivity without excluding every possible prognostic relationship. The patient-level healing contrast was imprecise, and the modest difference between exhaustive and add-one permutation probabilities did not change its interpretation. Paired anatomical differences were detectable, but tissue sensitivity alone does not establish healing specificity. These distinctions matter when cell-level observations are translated into claims about patients \citep{squair2021,zimmerman2021,collins2015}.

The representation comparison is an internal audit of this cohort. Although outcome-dependent orientation and component selection were performed within training folds, the frozen topic encoder had already seen the discovery expression data. Bootstrap intervals on fixed out-of-fold scores omit uncertainty from repeating the full learning procedure, and the folds share training patients \citep{bengio2004}. Differences in observed AUC therefore do not establish a stable ranking of methods. Searching across representations, thresholds and topic capacities also requires independent evaluation before a preferred readout can be treated as a validated predictor \citep{cawley2010}.

External projections demonstrate that the frozen readout can be computed in additional wound contexts, but the cohorts do not share an independently adjudicated patient-level healing endpoint. Variation in sampling, cell yield and compartment definitions can contribute to inconsistent immune associations. These datasets cannot be pooled as interchangeable replications of a prognostic claim. The smallest projection, with two specimens, is informative about computational feasibility rather than association strength.

A future validation study should specify the patient outcome and clinically meaningful difference before examining scores, preserve the patient as the independent unit and freeze the tissue-processing and analysis rules. The present power calculations depend on estimated discovery variances and approximate score distributions; they do not determine a guaranteed recruitment target. Likewise, the smallest margin compatible with an equivalence test is a description of these data, not a clinical threshold \citep{lakens2017}. Independent patient-level evaluation is needed to establish whether an interpretable tissue readout improves prognosis beyond the information already available at clinical assessment.
